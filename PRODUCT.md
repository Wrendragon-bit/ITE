# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Two readers, confirmed 2026-10-17:

- Recruiters, internship hosts and potential clients, who skim. They need to see who each person is, what they do, and how to reach them, without clicking through menus.
- A research department and other technical readers, who want depth on each project: problem, methods, results, stack, and links to code or papers.

Friends and classmates at RUPP come third. Voice is professional first.

## Product Purpose

Group portfolio for The 6 Crusaders, six Information Technology Engineering (ITE) students at the Royal University of Phnom Penh (RUPP). Success: within seconds a visitor knows who the six are, finds the person whose specialty they need, and contacts that person directly.

## Positioning

Six people with six distinct specialties (startup strategy, security, cloud and trading, test automation, design, ML/AI), presented as one crew instead of six separate CVs.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site: HTML, CSS and a little vanilla JS, no build step. Files are edited and uploaded through the GitHub web UI.
- Hybrid structure (user-confirmed): one scrolling page carries the quick read, and each shipped project gets a full write-up on the same page with its own shareable address (#project-1), so a research department can be sent straight to it.
- Growth journey (user-confirmed 2026-10-17): the projects staircase becomes the crew's four years at RUPP, one step per year from Year 1 to Year 4. Each year lists up to four milestones of any kind (project, competition, internship, course), each naming the members involved; a shipped project's line links to its write-up. Years done are solid, years ahead dashed. One crew timeline, not six personal ones.
- Recruiter links (user-confirmed 2026-10-17): each member adds one professional link (LinkedIn, GitHub or a CV PDF), shown first on their card before the social links. No placeholder links: a card stays as it is until its member sends one.
- Every member has a name, role, focus, photo, phone, email and social links. Contact details are published on purpose and stay public.
- Open facts: the two old pages disagreed on spellings and roles (Proseth vs Proaseth; Koemhong as "Developer" vs "Mazer of A5"; Proseth as "Editor" vs "Design Architect"). Proseth's Telegram link duplicated Koemhong's (t.me/KaiZooKi) and needs confirming.

## Product Principles

1. People first: every section helps a visitor find and reach the right person.
2. Real over impressive: nothing invented; placeholders say they are placeholders.
3. One crew: six specialties read as a whole, not six disconnected CVs.
